Welcome to Neeky's Virtual Game Space

Welcome to my Virtual Game Space, a website dedicated to arcade games and older home-console titles. I created this website because video games have played an important role in my interests as both a player and an artist. Fighting games, role-playing games, platformers, Racing, rhythm, and other classic titles have introduced me to memorable characters, imaginative settings, expressive music, and distinctive visual styles. Many of these games continue to influence the way I think about character design, storytelling, color, and interactive experiences. This website is intended for longtime retro-game fans as well as newer players who may be curious about games from earlier generations. Visitors do not need to be experts or own a collection of older consoles to enjoy the site. My goal is to make these games approachable through clear information, personal observations, images, and recommendations. From the Lobby, visitors can enter the Game Collection to explore selected games from arcade machines and consoles such as the Nintendo Entertainment System, Super Nintendo, Sega Genesis, PlayStation, Sega Saturn, and Dreamcast. Each entry will include information about the game’s platform, genre, release period, and visual identity. Visitors can also use the Game Finder to receive a recommendation based on the type of experience they want. Neeky's Virtual Game Space does not attempt to document every game ever released. Instead, it presents a personal selection of titles that I consider visually interesting, unusual, or simply enjoyable. Choose your destination and discover a game worth exploring.
