Saturday night slam masters 2

Platforms: Arcade

Genre: Fighting

Ring of Destruction is the 1994 sequel to Saturday Night Slam Masters and the second game in the series. Unlike the first game, which played like a traditional wrestling game, Ring of Destruction's format was changed to play like a traditional one-on-one 2D fighting game (similar to Super Street Fighter II), with the action restricted to one plane. The game's configuration was changed to a five-button setup (two punch buttons, two kick buttons and a grappling button). The objective of each match is to deplete the opponent's life bar in two out of three rounds. It is no longer necessary for the player to pin their opponent to win a match, although all the other moves have been retained.

Rolling Thunder 2

Platforms: Arcade & Mega Drive

Genre: Side-scrolling shooter

Rolling Thunder 2 is a 1991 run and gun video game developed and published by Namco for arcades. It is the sequel to 1986's Rolling Thunder, retaining the same gameplay of its predecessor while adding cooperative gameplay for two players and improved graphics. Unlike the original, which was set in 1968, Rolling Thunder 2 has a more contemporary setting to go with its more futuristic design, as well as an optionally playable female character.
