Captain Commando

Platform: Arcade

Genre: Beat 'em up

The year is 2026, and the world is filled with crime. Captain Commando and his three faithful Commando Companions rise up to erase this crime from Planet Earth and from all the Galaxy, but the futuristic criminals they have to fight are endowed with secret hidden evil superpowers. Many of them are known as Super Criminals, with abilities beyond that of ordinary mere mortals. The leader of all the Super Criminals himself is a maniacal tyrannical alien known as Scumocide (known as Genocide in Japan).
